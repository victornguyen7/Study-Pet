            Apps and sites

              The time each app and site was in front, checked every 5 seconds: its name and the time only.                 Imports

                Read-only, checked every half hour. Canvas: open Calendar, then “Calendar Feed”. Google Calendar: open the calendar's settings, then copy “Secret address in iCal format”. Anyone with an address can read that calendar, so treat it like a password.

                No address? In Google Calendar open Settings, “Import & export”, then Export. Unzip the download and upload the .ics file. A file does not update itself: upload a new one when your calendar changes.

                Courses

                  Untick a course to hide its imported assignments. Nothing is deleted; tick it to bring them back.

                  What the pet has learned

                    Your answers about apps and sites. Forget one and the pet may ask again.

                    Focus keeper

                    During a focus block the pet glances at you after 30 seconds on something Relax, speaks after 2 minutes and sits in the middle of the screen after 5. Blocking is off unless you tick it, works in Chrome only, and covers the site's pages until the 5 minutes are over.

                    Permissions

                    Accessibility:

                    macOS asks for this before any app may read the title of the window in front. Study Pet will use the title to tell study from drift, such as which document is open. It will not read what is inside the window, and it cannot type or click for you. Titles are not read yet in this version. Without the permission the pet still sees which app is in front.

                    The button makes macOS show its own question, which opens System Settings, “Privacy & Security”, “Accessibility”. Turn Study Pet on there; turn it off there at any time.

                    Pet

                    Press Option Shift P to hide or show the pet. Drag it to move it.

                    Press Option Shift L and the pet looks away for 15 minutes: no app, page or drift is noted. Press it again to end that early.
